RF observation

What this is. The ESP32 listens to how Wi-Fi signals change in the room. This card says whether those changes repeat in a regular pattern.
  • State is the plain-language result. Data quality says whether the signal is good enough to trust it.
  • A regular pattern can come from a person, a fan or anything that moves. It is a reason to look, never proof of a person.
  • If quality is Low or None, ignore the state and fix the signal first.

Environmental sensor (experimental)

What this is. A gas sensor on the Arduino. The number is a raw electrical reading (0 to 1023), not a gas amount.
  • It needs about two minutes to warm up. After that the system learns what is normal here (the baseline).
  • Elevated means the reading rose well above that baseline. It does not say which gas, and it does not mean a person is present.
  • Press Re-baseline if you moved the sensor to a new place.

Incidents

What this is. An incident is a folder for evidence that deserves a human look.
  • It opens by itself when monitoring is on and something notable is seen, or you can open one with New incident.
  • Click an incident to read its evidence, add notes and Acknowledge it, which records that a person has looked.
  • Close incident only works after it was acknowledged. New evidence reopens an acknowledged incident.
  • Request inspection logs your request and shows a message on the Arduino screen.

    No incidents yet. They open when monitoring is on and an observation is recorded, or when an operator opens one.
